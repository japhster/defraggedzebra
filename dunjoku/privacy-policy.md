# Dunjoku Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 8 October 2026

Dunjoku is a puzzle game developed by DefraggedZebra ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

Dunjoku does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game works entirely offline.

## Information we collect

None. Dunjoku does not:

- ask you to create an account or sign in
- connect to the internet or send data to any server
- include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services
- request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other device permission

## Data stored on your device

Dunjoku saves your game progress, such as best completion times, daily puzzle history, and streaks, in a file stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes it.

## Children's privacy

Because Dunjoku collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Third parties

Dunjoku is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information in connection with your use of the Play Store under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive any personal information from Google about you.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Dunjoku changes how data is handled, for example by adding in-app purchases, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
